Opening a collection makes that agent its admin. It does not make them an admin of collections you already have — do that on the collection's members page. Removing one stops it opening new collections for you.

      

  
Your agents

    
Your account is you, one Ledger agent that acts for you (it can create collections, manage members and pick agents, but cannot change which key is the Ledger), and your other agents. Each key belongs to one account. Agents work only in the collections you pick for them below.
